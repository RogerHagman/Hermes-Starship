# ORD-1274 · Light up the Holodeck station picture

Branch: holo/ORD-1274 · Version 0.2.23

## What changed

- frontend/tools/make_deck_stations_svg.py, holo(): the picture is now drawn in two layers.
  - The dark room underneath never dims: the faint gold grid at the old strengths (.35 / .3 / .18),
    the far-wall opening and the dark arch frame.
  - On top, in groups carrying the GLOW style (opacity: var(--sa-glow, 1)), the "running" layer:
    - a warm amber light spilling from the arch over the room, plus a soft pool on the floor;
    - the grid again, brighter (.75 floor rays / .65 cross lines / .4 wall and ceiling, 1.2 px);
    - the far-wall opening glowing warm, brighter at the bottom;
    - the arch edge with a wide soft halo and a bright 2.4 px gold edge (was 2 px at .7);
    - the cube: a cool glow behind it, faintly filled faces, a soft halo on every edge and 2 px edges
      (was 1.6 px bare wire), with a small pool of light on the floor under it;
    - the arch panel's big key a bit brighter (.55, was .35), and now wrapped in a GLOW group so it
      really dims off duty (see open questions).
  - The grid code is now one small helper, grid(), called twice at two strengths.
- frontend/src/assets/station-holo.svg regenerated with `python3 frontend/tools/make_deck_stations_svg.py`.
  All other station SVGs came out byte-identical (fixed seeds), so only this one is in the diff.
- No React or CSS changes. The cube still floats with the existing sa-holo animation (transform only).

## Which board

Deck 4, the Holodeck station (station id holo), wherever its station picture shows: the deck view
card and the Holodeck board header.

## What to look at on screen

1. Holodeck station, on duty: the room should read as switched on: a bright gold grid running
   to the opening in the far wall, a warm amber glow inside the arch, and the cube clearly lit in
   the Claude colour with a glow round it, gently floating.
2. Put the station off duty (or compare with another off-duty station): the warm light, bright
   grid, arch halo and cube should all fade with --sa-glow, leaving the dark room with its faint grid,
   much as the picture looked before this order.
3. Check at 2560×1440 that the cube and arch still sit in the middle of the crop (nothing moved).

## Open questions

- The sa-key class (bcarm animation) animates opacity, and a CSS animation beats the inline GLOW
  opacity on the same element, so a blinking key never dimmed off duty. Fixed here for the Holodeck
  by moving GLOW onto a group round the key. Other stations' sa-key / sa-onair lights still have the
  old pattern; worth a separate order to give them the same wrapper.
- Glow strengths are a judgement call from the code; I could not render the picture here (no build).
  If it reads too bright next to the other Deck 4 picture (Stellar Cartography), the numbers to turn
  down are the grid() call strengths and the hd-warm gradient's first stop (.22).
