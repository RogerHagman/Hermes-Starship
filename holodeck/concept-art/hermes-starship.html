Meet the crew

Each station gets a character in a two-tone uniform. The tunic shows the rail it thinks on, and only the Captain wears gold. The ring under its feet shows its status: solid for live, dashed for proposed, dotted for future hires, who appear only as holograms until the captain approves them.

LOUNGEMINUTES

The first briefing: how to build the ship

The idea doc leaves the stack, the hosting, the look and the data flow open on purpose. Here the crew meets round the plotting table to work through them. Each station speaks to its part, the Ship's Computer keeps the minutes on the local rail, and nothing is decided until the captain says so. A made-up session that plays on a loop.
